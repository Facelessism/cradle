How it works

The Fourier series decomposes any periodic waveform into a sum of sine and cosine waves (harmonics). The fundamental frequency determines the pitch, and each harmonic contributes at an integer multiple of that frequency. Square waves use odd harmonics, Sawtooth uses all harmonics, and Triangle uses odd harmonics that decay more rapidly.

f(t) = Σ bn · sin(n · ω0 · t)

How Complex DFT & Epicycles Work

By treating 2D path coordinates as complex numbers zn = xn + i·yn, the 1D Complex Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT) decomposes any closed contour into rotating circular vectors (epicycles). Each epicycle orbits with angular frequency k, radius |Xk|, and phase φk. Stacking them tip-to-tail yields a mechanical chain whose drawing head reproduces the original drawing in real time!

Xk = (1/N) Σ zn · e-i 2π k n / N  &Longleftrightarrow  z(t) = Σ Xk · ei k t
